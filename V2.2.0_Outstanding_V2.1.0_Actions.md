## 5. No Metric Fabrication

**Important**: These carry-forward items are **not** included in health score calculations or "completion percentages" intentionally. They are tracked as operational governance items with clear ownership and deadlines, but they do not:

- ✅ Count toward or against repository health scores
- ✅ Improve or degrade numerical metrics when resolved
- ✅ Replace the V2.1.0 conditional completion verdict
- ❌ Are fabricated or artificially inflated
- ❌ Are used to claim "V2.2.0 completes V2.1.0 pending items"

**Rationale**: V2.1.0 concluded CONDITIONALLY COMPLETE with these items documented as maintainer-dependent. V2.2.0 carries them forward with the same transparency. Resolving or not resolving these items does not change the V2.1.0 verdict, nor should they be used to claim progress they cannot deliver.

**V2.2.0 Position**: Carry-forward items are managed as sustainable operational governance, not as numerical metric improvement targets.
